Constraint Satisfaction Problem (CSP) Formulation

Formal Model: A timetable is defined by a tuple (X, D, C) where:

  • Variables (X): Every lecture session and laboratory block across all registered courses.
  • Domains (D): Feasible assignments of (Day, Start Slot, Room) satisfying time and capacity boundaries.
  • Hard Constraints (C_hard): Absolute inviolable constraints. Solutions violating even one hard constraint are discarded.
  • Soft Constraints (C_soft): Quality optimization metrics aggregated into a weighted soft score.

The 6 Hard Constraints

  1. Room Conflict: At most one academic session per room per time slot.
  2. Faculty Conflict: No instructor scheduled in two simultaneous classes.
  3. Cohort Conflict: No student section or overlapping lab batch double-booked.
  4. Faculty Availability: Sessions scheduled only during designated available days and hours.
  5. Room Suitability: Laboratories assigned exclusively to lab blocks; seating capacity ≥ cohort strength.
  6. Session Continuity: Multi-hour lab blocks assigned to contiguous unbroken time slots.
